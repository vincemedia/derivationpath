Your coins never left the blockchain. They're waiting... deep inside a cave.
Introducing Derivation Path.
Your seed phrase is the password. Say all twelve words, in the right order... and the stone door rolls open.
But inside, it's pitch dark. There are thousands of tunnels, and your coins sit in just a few of them.
The derivation path is your torch. It's a list of turns, like 44, 0, 0, that lights the one trail from the door to your coins.
Every chamber along the trail is one of your addresses, with your coins and their history.
Here's the catch: every wallet app lit its own trail. Same password, different turns... so the wrong path shows an empty cave, even when your coins are right there.
Derivation Path knows the trails of Centbee, RockWallet, ElectrumSV and more. Pick your old wallet, and enter your phrase.
Not sure which one? It walks every trail it knows, until it finds only empty chambers, and lists every address with coins.
Then move them into your BRC-100 wallet, or to any address. You check every transaction before it's sent.
It all runs in your browser. Your phrase never leaves your device.
Derivation Path. Find your way back to your coins.
