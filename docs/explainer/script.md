# Derivation Path: the explainer, scene by scene

What's on screen, with times (from `python3 video/render.py --timings`). The words are in `video/voiceover.json` and, with their timecodes, in `voiceover.md`. 9:16, 720×1280, 1:51.

**The idea:** your seed phrase is the password to a secret cave. Inside it's dark, with thousands of tunnels. The derivation path is the torch-lit trail, a list of turns, from the door to the chambers where your coins are: your addresses. Every wallet app lit its own trail, so the wrong one shows an empty cave. Derivation Path knows the trails.
